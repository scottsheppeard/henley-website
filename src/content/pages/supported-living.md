# Live a life you'll _love_

## Find less stress and more support at The Henley.

Keep your independence with customised care. Living at The Henley, you can feel comfortable and confident in your home. Whether you need temporary assistance, or something longer term, all you have to do is ask.

[Contact Us](/contact/)

## Your life, _your_ way...

## It's all about you.

Everyone is unique. Your support will be too. Our team will get to know you – your interests, goals and dreams – crafting a customised care plan to suit your situation.

Enjoy as much or as little support as you want, tapping into your tailor-made plan as needed.

Get Support with:

-   Personal care
-   Cleaning
-   Cooking
-   Shopping
-   Transport
-   Nursing Care

## Support at every stage.

Your needs may change. Your lifestyle doesn’t have to.

Our team offers flexible, affordable care that’s scalable to your needs. In your apartment or elsewhere on site, you’ll get swift support in ten-minute increments – ready for extra time if required.

Our Care Concierge is always available to make life easier. Let us support you by:

-   Coordinating your services
-   Organising prescriptions
-   Managing your administration
-   Communicating with your family.

## Need a helping hand? With a minimum of two qualified staff on-call 24/7, support is never far away.
