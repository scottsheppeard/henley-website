Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Henley on Broadwater?

The Henley on Broadwater is a private luxury lifestyle resort like no other!

Where is The Henley on Broadwater?

It’s located in one of the Gold Coast’s most prestigious precincts, The Broadwater. A short stroll to restaurants, Gold Coast Aquatic Centre, Broadwater Parklands with 16km of walking and cycling paths, buses and the Light Rail plus a mere 300m walk to Australia Fair Shopping Centre.

What does The Henley on Broadwater offer?

A unique, secure, low maintenance lifestyle located in the heart of the Gold Coast. An opportunity to have the privacy and security of your own home with the added benefit of 5 star facilities with support and care if ever required.

Does The Henley on Broadwater have security?

Yes. The resort has 24 hour security and is fitted with intercoms, discrete security cameras and security access to the main and other ancillary entrances. The Henley on Broadwater apartments are secure, combining intercoms, telephones and electronic remote control garages. The resort is professionally managed, so your home will be safe whether you’re at home or away travelling.

Am I eligible to purchase and live at The Henley on Broadwater?

In summary, to purchase and live at The Henley on Broadwater, you must be of retirement age and of good nature. Eligibility is by way of approval to ensure our community is a happy one.

What facilities are provided?

Outstanding amenities make The Henley on Broadwater an exceptional all-encompassing lifestyle address. It features The Broadwater Bistro along with in-room dining, a hair and beauty salon, a surround sound cinema, an outdoor heated pool and spa and a fully equipped gym. Utilise the games room or the recreation centre with a snooker table or take advantage of the library with internet services, outdoor barbeque terraces, the community lounge or the bar and entertainment lounge. You can even grow your own organic vegetables in the sky deck community garden.

Is there convenient shopping available nearby?

Yes. The Henley on Broadwater is situated just a short stroll from Australia Fair Shopping Centre which includes supermarkets, a range of specialty shops, cafes and restaurants. Surfers Paradise and Pacific Fair shopping centres are also easily accessed by the Gold Coast light rail from Southport.

Can family and friends stay with me?

Yes, friends and family are most welcome. We know your friends and family will enjoy visiting you at your new home and enjoying all the fabulous facilities on offer at The Henley on Broadwater.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes, pets are very welcome at The Henley.

Who is responsible for the maintenance of the building and resort?

The business owners of The Henley maintain the building and the external areas of the apartments. In addition, all the facilities are maintained to a resort quality standard and audited yearly by the Retirement Village Act to ensure this is the case.

Is there any public transport nearby?

Yes, it’s a short stroll to local buses and the Gold Coast Light Rail.

Can I rent at The Henley on Broadwater?

No. To ensure the integrity of our community, we only allow homes to be owner-occupied.

What kind of contract do I enter into when I buy an apartment at The Henley?

The contract you sign when you purchase your home is legislated under the Retirement Villages Act. The contract provides you with security of tenure under Queensland Government Legislation and is registered with the QLD Lands Act.

Are there trained personal carers onsite 24 hours a day, 7 days a week should I need them?

Yes. The Henley on Broadwater have on-site trained personal carers managed by a team of registered nurses who are available 24 hours a day 7 days a week.

Are there additional medical facilities nearby?

Yes. The Henley on Broadwater is in the heart of Southport, which has a choice of medical clinics, dentists, pathologists, optometrists, chemists, leading health care practitioners and hospitals.

What are the weekly fees?

The weekly fee for a one-bedroom apartment is $168.60 per week, a two-bedroom apartment is $174.67 per week, a three-bedroom apartment is $220.18 per week, and a four-bedroom apartment is $250.52 per week.

What do the weekly fees cover?

A weekly fee is payable by you to The Henley on Broadwater. The fee covers the cost of the resort management personnel and staff as well as the maintenance of all common areas, use and maintenance of all the facilities.

How do I pay my weekly fees?

Weekly fees are charged monthly by way of direct debit from your nominated bank account.

Can the weekly fees increase?

It may increase, the weekly fees are budgeted annually and capped by CPI or by market review.

What about other costs?

You are responsible for normal services such as electricity, telephone, gas for your hot water and home contents insurance.

Do I pay Stamp Duty when I buy an apartment at The Henley?

No. Stamp Duty does not apply to apartments at The Henley on Broadwater.

What is the quality of the resort?

The Henley on Broadwater was built by leading builders and designed by award winning architects. The Henley on Broadwater looks and feels like a 5 star resort.

Are the apartments protected against termites and pests?

Yes. The building is protected from termites and pests in accordance with council building regulations and Australian Standards.

Can I sell my apartment?

Yes, at any time, either we can assist you in selling your home or you can appoint an external selling agent. However the home must be sold to an approved purchaser.
