# Relax into luxury living on the Gold Coast 

## Leave behind the daily grind and retire in style. 

Positioned on the doorstep of the Broadwater Parklands – with stunning ocean views – The Henley is known for location and experience. 

Eat-in at the on-site bistro

Get pampered at our hair and beauty room

Catch up and share a wine at one of our events

Challenge yourself on the Sky Deck bowling green.

On offer are 1, 2 3 & 4 bedroom independent living apartments.
Ready to retire in luxury? Contact us to book your tour.

## Now’s your time to relax and indulge... 

## A resort-style concierge experience. 

With so much to do at The Henley, it can be hard to coax yourself out the front door. The luxury standards you’ll find in your apartment extend to the social and community areas too.
Make the most of in-house facilities including:
Swimming pool and gym
Snooker table
Rooftop Bowls
Community Garden
Library
Craft Room
Men’s Shed
Communal BBQ area
Entertainment Lounge and bar 

## Connect with community. 

Define your own retirement at The Henley on Broadwater. Whether you’re a homebody, a fine-dining-foodie, or love to stroll on the beach, you’ll find an easier life and the space to rediscover you.
Enjoy our packed events calendar with something for everyone. These are the catch ups where you’ll meet new neighbours who will become fast friends.
Every month there’s new themed events and leisure activities to look forward to including:
St Patrick’s Day
Melbourne Cup
Christmas
Shows
Casino Night 

## There’s always time for casual BBQ catchups, a game of lawn bowls, or a meal at the onsite bistro.
