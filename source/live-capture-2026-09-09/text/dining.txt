# Haute cuisine at home 

## Luxury meals just minutes from home 

Indulge the senses in our sensational 70 Marine Bistro. Leave the planning, cooking and cleaning up to us – all you need to do is dine.
Enjoy a meal alone, with family and friends – or maybe meet someone new. Whatever you choose, you’ll find friendly smiles, attentive service and fresh cuisine to treat your taste buds.
Hungry for more? Contact us to book your tour.

## Taste The World 

## Take a trip across the globe with our regular themed dinners. Inhale the aroma of Tuscan treats, savour the smells of Saigon streets or feast on traditional French fare. 

## No passport required. Just book a place at the table. 

## Excellence - made easy 

Pop in for a quick bite to eat mid-week, or stay for a long, lazy weekend meal. Every experience is prepared and delivered to exacting standards.
So you can expect the best. And leave the rest to us. 

## Relax with room service 

Not feeling social? No stress. Our menu is prepared on demand by professional chefs. Simply choose from the a la carte menu and we’ll bring your meal to you.
