# Our fashionistas at The Henley 

On 12 April, [The Henley on Broadwater] held a fashion parade hosted by iconic Gold Coast fashion designer Margot Mott. There were so many fabulous designed, colours and textures.

The fashion parade is a fabulous way to catch up with friends and neighbours.
