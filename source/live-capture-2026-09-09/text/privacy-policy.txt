# Privacy Policy 

In compliance with the Australian Privacy Act (1988), and the Australian Privacy Principles (2014), this website may collect and store personal contact information, such as name, phone and email address to provide you with information as requested via the contact form, as well as sending through service-related information and marketing updates that may be of interest to the consumer.

We do not give or sell personal contact information to any other third parties unless given express permission.

Other website links are out of our control or ownership and advise the consumer to read the Privacy Policies for each site they visit.

The security of your Personal Information is important to us but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
