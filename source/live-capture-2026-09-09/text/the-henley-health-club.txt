# Vibrant, active, healthy and happy 

## Seize the day, every day at 
The Henley Health Club 

Retire your old routine and experience the latest in wellbeing. A light stroll from anywhere in the resort, The Henley Health Club has everything you need to stay fit, healthy, active and independent.

Whether you’re a life-long gym junkie or just starting your journey, exercise is essential for mental and physical wellness. And we’re committed to supporting you every step of the way.

Raise your heart-rate – and your spirits – with:

Yoga

Meditation

Aqua Aerobics

State of the art gym

Chair Meditation

Walking Groups

Balance Class

Gentle Exercise Class

Cardio Exercise Class

Our personal trainers understand everyone is different. That’s why they’ll never take the ‘one-size-fits-all’ approach.
