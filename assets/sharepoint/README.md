# SharePoint image picks for thehenley.com.au

Pulled read-only from `blockbuster.sharepoint.com/sites/DocumentCenter` (library `Documents`) on 2026-09-10. Originals, original filenames, grouped by suggested website use. 93 files, 518 MB.

Full folder inventory: `sharepoint-inventory.md`. Contact sheets of ~1,440 candidate images (46 sheets, 6x8 grid, numbered) are in `_contact-sheets/` with `index.json` / `index.csv` mapping every `#number` to its SharePoint drive item id, path, size and dimensions, so any image on a sheet can be fetched later by number.

## Privacy / licensing caveats

- Anything in `lifestyle/`, `aged-care/`, `hubstitute-2026/`, `staff/`, `health-club/AR405266.JPG` and `amenities/_MG_0790.jpg` shows identifiable residents or staff. The 2015/2016/2019 images came from marketing shoots (Brand & Co April 2015; Oct 2016 lifestyle shoot; 2019 newsletter/aged-care shoot) but consent records were not checked - the folder `Sales and Marketing/.../Brand & Co - April 2015/DO NOT USE - no consent` shows consent was tracked, so confirm before publishing faces, and expect some of these residents to have moved on or passed away in the last 7-11 years. The Hubstitute 2026 set is the safest (recent, Scott's own photos, current staff) but still contains residents.
- `aerials-broadwater/Aerial_renew-license-05.05.2016.jpg` is a licensed third-party aerial (licence renewal noted in filename) - check the licence before web use. The `_53a*` aerials appear to be the 2015 commissioned shoot (same photographer sequence as the apartment shots).
- `exteriors/Building.jpg` looks like a CGI/night render, not a photograph.
- Stock images in `General/Photo library/Stock images` (iStock/Shutterstock) were NOT downloaded - licence status unknown.
- `Resident Head Shots` and tenancy-condition photo folders were deliberately excluded.
- RAW originals: Scott's gym shoot (`General/Photo library/Scotts RAW images/210624 Gym`) has Sony .ARW + .xmp alongside the JPGs; `Sales and Marketing/Images/Christmas 2022` also has ARW. Brand & Co 2015 exists in Low Res / Hi Res / Print res JPEG copies (print-res chosen where available). No PSD originals of photos were found (PSDs are logo/flyer artwork only).
